Teams Message Extractor - Chat Export

Everything happens in your browser. Your messages and files are processed locally on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party server. See the privacy policy for details.

Export Microsoft Teams chat messages, files, images and emoji reactions to CSV or HTML. Scrolls the full chat history automatically for a complete backup and archive, with attachments bundled as a single ZIP.

Teams Message Extractor automatically scrolls through your full conversation history, from the very first message to the latest, and exports everything to a clean, structured CSV file or a self-contained HTML transcript. File attachments download as one ZIP archive instead of dozens of save prompts. Works with direct messages, group chats, and channels across all three Teams domains (teams.microsoft.com, teams.live.com, teams.cloud.microsoft).

What's new in 2.9.0

  • New: channel (Posts) extraction. Threaded channel conversations export with every nested reply, subject line and thread structure included.
  • New Markdown export, ideal for pasting into notes, wikis or AI tools.
  • CSV export now opens correctly in Excel. Accented and international characters (é, ã, ü, ç) are no longer garbled.
  • Pasted images and screenshots are now captured reliably, including on the new teams.cloud.microsoft layout where many were previously missed.
  • Screenshots are embedded directly in the HTML transcript, so images appear in the export itself rather than as links.
  • Attachments save as the exact original file at original quality, with no recompression.

What gets captured

  • Sender names & timestamps
  • Full message content
  • Emoji reactions (per message, with counts)
  • File attachments, images, and their URLs
  • Complete conversation threads

How it works

  1. Open any Teams conversation in your browser
  2. Click the extension icon
  3. The extension auto-scrolls to the very beginning of the chat, capturing every message
  4. Download as CSV, HTML, or a single bundled ZIP

Perfect for

  • Archiving important conversations before they're lost
  • Compliance and audit requirements
  • Searching through old chat history offline
  • Backing up project discussions and decisions
  • Migrating data or creating records
  • HR and legal documentation needs

Key features

  • Automatic scrolling captures your entire chat history, no manual scrolling needed
  • Works everywhere in Teams: direct messages, group chats, channels, and all three Teams domains
  • Multiple export formats: CSV for spreadsheets, HTML for a formatted transcript with reactions and clickable attachment links
  • Streaming ZIP downloads bundle every attachment into one archive, with ZIP64 support for archives larger than 4 GB and DEFLATE compression for text files
  • Emoji reactions are captured per message and shown in both CSV and HTML
  • Downloads carry on by themselves: close the popup and the ZIP keeps streaming to disk in the background
  • Private by design: all processing happens locally in your browser and no data is sent to external servers
  • Available in 20 languages with right-to-left support for Arabic and Urdu
  • Lightweight and fast
