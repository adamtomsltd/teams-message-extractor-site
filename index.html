Teams Message Extractor — Chat Export

A browser extension that exports Microsoft Teams chat messages — including files, images, and emoji reactions — to CSV or HTML, with attachments bundled as a single ZIP. It auto-scrolls your full conversation history so nothing is missed.

Everything happens in your browser. Your messages and files are processed locally on your device and are never sent to us or to any third-party server. See the privacy policy for details.

Key features

  • Full auto-scroll capture of the entire chat history
  • CSV and self-contained HTML transcript exports
  • File attachments and pasted screenshots, bundled into one streaming ZIP
  • Emoji reactions and quoted-message context preserved
  • Available in 20 languages, switchable at runtime
  • Works on teams.microsoft.com, teams.live.com, and teams.cloud.microsoft
