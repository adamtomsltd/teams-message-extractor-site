Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026 · Applies to the "Teams Message Extractor — Chat Export" browser extension

Summary: everything the extension does happens locally in your browser. Your Teams messages, files, and exports never leave your device. We collect no analytics, run no servers, and see none of your data.

What the extension does with your data

When you start an extraction, the extension reads the Microsoft Teams conversation that is open in your browser tab and builds the export (CSV, HTML, or ZIP of attachments) entirely on your device. The resulting files are saved through your browser's normal download mechanism to your computer. Message content is never transmitted to the extension's developer or to any third party.

What is stored, and where

  • Settings (language preference, scroll delay, export options) are stored in your browser's local extension storage.
  • Extraction results are kept temporarily in session storage so the popup can restore its state if you close and reopen it. They are automatically cleared after 30 minutes, or when the tab closes.
  • A first-install timestamp is stored locally so that, if a paid tier launches in the future, long-time users can be recognised for an early-supporter discount. It never leaves your device.

What is NOT collected

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind
  • No account, sign-up, or personal information
  • No message content, file content, contact names, or metadata sent anywhere
  • No cookies, no fingerprinting, no advertising identifiers

Why the extension requests its permissions

  • Teams domains (teams.microsoft.com, teams.live.com, teams.cloud.microsoft) — to read the open conversation so it can be exported.
  • SharePoint / OneDrive / Teams media hosts (sharepoint.com, 1drv.ms, asm.skype.com and related) — to download the file attachments and images that appear in your chats, using your existing signed-in session. These downloads go directly from Microsoft's servers to your device.
  • Storage — for the settings and temporary state listed above.
  • Downloads — to save your exports to your computer.

Future paid features

A paid tier is planned for a future update (see the Paid Model FAQ). If and when it launches, activating a licence will send only your licence key and a random, anonymous installation identifier to our payment and licensing provider, Lemon Squeezy, to validate the licence. Payment details are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy under their privacy policy; the extension never sees your payment information. Your Teams message content will continue to be processed only on your device. This policy will be updated before any such change takes effect.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the extension's release notes and reflected on this page with a new effective date.
