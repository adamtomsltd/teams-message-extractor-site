The upcoming paid model

Last updated: 12 July 2026 — details below will be filled in as they are confirmed.

The short version: the extension will move to a paid model in a future update — not yet, and not without warning. You'll get advance notice here and in the extension, a 1-month free grace period, and existing users will get a discounted early-supporter price.

When is this happening?

No date is set yet. When one is, it will be announced in the extension itself and on this page well in advance, followed by a full month where everything keeps working free of charge.

Will I lose anything I have today?

The intent is that the features you rely on today keep working. The exact free/paid split will be published on this page before anything changes.

What will paid features look like?

The paid tier is planned around new capabilities — richer export formats and power features — rather than taking away what's already free. Specifics and pricing will appear here once confirmed.

How will existing users be recognised?

The extension locally records when it was first installed. Installs that pre-date the announcement will be offered the discounted early-supporter price automatically. Nothing about this leaves your device — see the privacy policy.
